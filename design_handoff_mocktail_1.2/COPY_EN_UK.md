# Mocktail Finder 1.2 — copy (EN / UA)

Every visible string. Prices are not here: they always come from StoreKit. `{n}`, `{x}`, `{a}`, `{b}` are placeholders. Plurals: English one / other; Ukrainian 1 / 2–4 / 5+ (1, 21, 31… → first form; 2–4, 22–24… → second; the rest, including 11–14 → third). Drink names, ingredients and steps live in `data/recipes.json`.
